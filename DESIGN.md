# Design System: VRP Plan

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Harbor Briefing"**

VRP Plan presents as a dusk-harbor desk: a deep navy field, a quiet wordmark, and Inter set with the restraint of an operations brief. The public canvas persuades without theatre — no second brand mark, no decorative kicker, no invented social proof. Paper cards and a single navy voice do the work.

Two surfaces share one system. Marketing (the design-priority surface) is a prospect briefing: sticky frosted chrome, a two-column hero, glass-edged feature cards, and a navy close. The signed-in shell reuses the same navy, Inter, radii, and buttons for daily operation; it is not the surface this system is optimized for.

The material is professional and slightly maritime — cool paper, mist borders, modest downward shadows — aligned with the EduProf navy lineage. Imagery is documentary (the login photograph as a hero illustration), never a logo.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One navy voice; paper and frost as the only counter-materials
- Wordmark chrome; no photographic header mark
- Inter only, loaded at 400 / 500 / 600 / 700
- Modest corners and soft offset shadows
- Dark-canvas primaries invert to paper; the closing band stays light-on-navy

## Colors

A single navy family, from ink to chart, on cool paper. No second brand hue.

### Primary
- **Harbor Navy** (`{colors.primary}`): The action color. Filled buttons, the default primary token, and the deep stop of the CTA band.
- **Dockyard Blue** (`{colors.primary-hover}`): Hover on filled buttons; the light stop of the CTA band gradient.
- **Chart Blue** (`{colors.accent}`): Links on paper (auth footnotes) and the mid-navy of the family. Not a competing accent for new surfaces.
- **Crest Navy** (`{colors.logo-bg}`): Signed-in sidebar mark tile only. Not a public header color.
- **Night Beacon** (`{colors.dark-primary}`): Dark-theme text accent and sidebar highlight. Filled buttons stay Harbor Navy even in dark mode.

### Neutral
- **Cool Paper** (`{colors.surface-page}`): App page wash; light-theme marketing field when the visitor chooses light.
- **Frost Card** (`{colors.surface-card}`): Feature cards (light theme) and auth paper.
- **Glass Paper** (`{colors.surface-container-lowest}`): Secondary button fill; lightest stacked surface.
- **Quiet Gray** (`{colors.surface-container-low}`): Secondary hover and disabled input wash.
- **Night Dock** (`{colors.surface-nav}`): Signed-in sidebar.
- **Night Paper** (`{colors.dark-surface}`): Dark page and inverted-button ink.
- **Night Card** (`{colors.dark-card}`): Dark-theme cards.
- **Input White** (`{colors.surface-input}`): Field interiors on paper.
- **Ink Navy** (`{colors.text-heading}`): Headings on paper; light-theme marketing titles.
- **Slate Ink** (`{colors.text-body}`): Body copy on paper.
- **Harbor Slate** (`{colors.text-secondary}`): Secondary labels and default secondary-button text.
- **Quiet Slate** (`{colors.text-subtle}`): Captions and feature-card body.
- **Mist Line** (`{colors.border-subtle}`): Default hairline.
- **Signal White** (`{colors.on-primary}` / `{colors.marketing-text}`): Type and icons on navy; default marketing text on the dark canvas.
- **Soft Signal** (`{colors.marketing-text-muted}`): Marketing lead copy on the dark canvas.
- **Inverted Paper** (`{colors.invert-cta-bg}`): Primary fill when a control sits on navy (dark header/hero, always the CTA band).
- **Inverted Ink** (`{colors.invert-cta-fg}`): Text on Inverted Paper.

### Semantic
- **Ledger Green** (`{colors.feedback-success}`): Confirmation status on marketing forms.
- **Alert Crimson** (`{colors.feedback-danger}`): Errors and destructive actions.

### Named Rules
**The Harbor Ink Rule.** Harbor Navy is the only brand voice of action. Do not introduce a second filled hue.

**The Canvas Invert Rule.** On the dark marketing canvas, header and hero primaries flip to Inverted Paper on Inverted Ink. The closing CTA band always keeps a light primary on navy, in every theme.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** Inter (same stack; no mono face in the system)

**Character:** A single neo-grotesque, slightly condensed at display by negative tracking. Professional French product voice; the type does not perform.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, `clamp(2.15rem, 4.4vw, 3.15rem)`, 1.08, −0.03em): Marketing hero title only. Stands alone — no line above it.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2rem)`): Section titles and the CTA-band heading (`clamp(1.35rem, 2.5vw, 1.75rem)` there).
- **Title** (700, 1.5rem): Auth card headings. Feature-card titles sit slightly smaller (1.05rem, 700).
- **Wordmark** (700, 1.125rem, −0.02em): Public header product name.
- **Lead** (400, `clamp(1rem, 2vw, 1.2rem)`, 1.6): Hero and section supporting copy; cap near 36–38rem.
- **Body** (400, 0.875rem, 1.5): Default document size. Feature-card body is 0.9375rem / 1.55.
- **Button** (600, 0.8125rem, 0.01em): Every shared control.
- **Label** (600, 0.6875rem, 0.06em, uppercase): Form field labels.

Inter is served from fonts.bunny.net at 400 / 500 / 600 / 700. Display asks for 800; the loaded cut tops out at 700.

### Named Rules
**The Bare Headline Rule.** The hero title stands alone. No kicker, eyebrow, or category line above it.

**The One Family Rule.** Inter carries every role. Do not pair a second display face.

## Layout

Marketing content lives in a centered column (`min(72rem, 100%)`) with vertical padding of `{spacing.spacing-6}` / `{spacing.spacing-8}`. The hero is one column until 900px, then a 1.05 / 0.95 split. Feature cards are an auto-fit grid that locks to three columns at 900px. Below 640px, header text links hide and hero actions go full-width.

The header is a sticky frosted bar: wordmark left, theme control and actions right, `{spacing.spacing-3}` / `{spacing.spacing-6}` padding. A skip link sits above the fold and appears on focus.

Public page regions are isolated from signed-in section chrome. Marketing sections do not pick up the app’s frosted panel, list, or header treatments.

The signed-in shell (lower design priority) is a fixed navy sidebar (16.25rem expanded, 4.5rem compact) plus a 4rem frosted topbar. App content maxes at `{--container-max}` (90rem). Spacing follows the `{spacing.spacing-1}`–`{spacing.spacing-12}` scale (0.25rem–3rem).

### Named Rules
**The Isolated Canvas Rule.** Public pages do not inherit signed-in section chrome. Marketing regions stay flush and unboxed.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid: tonal navy (the canvas, the sidebar, the CTA band) plus a small set of soft offset shadows. Nothing is flat-by-default and nothing uses a zero-offset halo. Frosted chrome (`blur(12px)` on the header, `blur(8px)` on feature cards) is a marketing-only veil, not a global glass language.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting lift** (`0 1px 3px 0 #0f172a0d`): Inputs and compact resting surfaces.
- **Card lift** (`0 4px 14px -3px #0f172a12, 0 2px 4px -2px #0f172a0a`): Feature cards.
- **Stage lift** (`0 12px 28px -6px #0f172a18, 0 4px 8px -4px #0f172a0d`): Hero illustration, auth cards, CTA band.
- **Primary press** (`0 1px 2px #00236f1a, 0 4px 14px #00236f26`; hover deepens to `0 2px 4px #00236f1f, 0 8px 20px #00236f33`): Filled buttons on paper.
- **Inverted press** (`0 1px 2px #00000040, 0 8px 20px #00000033`): Primaries sitting on navy.

Dark theme shadows shift to black-alpha equivalents of the same offsets. Feature cards do not lift further on hover.

### Named Rules
**The Offset Lift Rule.** Depth is a modest downward offset plus blur. Never a zero-offset glow halo.

## Shapes

Corners are modest and stepped. Fields and chrome chips use the medium radius (`{rounded.rounded-md}`, 0.375rem). Cards, the hero photograph, and the CTA band use the large radius (`{rounded.rounded-lg}`, 0.5rem). Default buttons use the extra-large radius (`{rounded.rounded-xl}`, 0.75rem); compact header actions drop to large. No pills, no square tiles, no clipped diagonals.

Hairlines are 1px Mist Line on paper, 1px white at 10–15% on navy. The hero photograph is 520 / 360, covered, large-radius, stage-lifted — an illustration, not a mark.

Stroke icons (1.75px, rounded caps) sit in a 2.5rem medium-radius well. They are the only icon language.

### Named Rules
**The Modest Corner Rule.** Cards and images use the large radius. Default buttons use the extra-large radius. No pills, no squares.

## Components

Controls are shared and tactile: a 2.5rem minimum, 600-weight Inter, a 150ms color shift, and a 2% press on filled actions. Focus is a 3px Harbor-tinted ring on paper, or a 2px Signal White outline on the dark canvas.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Extra-large radius (0.75rem) at default; large (0.5rem) when compact. Padding 0.625rem 1.125rem (compact 0.5rem 0.875rem).
- **Primary:** Harbor Navy on Signal White, with the primary-press shadow. Hover moves to Dockyard Blue.
- **Primary invert:** Inverted Paper on Inverted Ink — dark-theme header and hero, and every CTA-band primary.
- **Secondary:** Glass Paper, Harbor Slate, Mist Line border, no shadow. Hover: Quiet Gray, Ink Navy, slightly deeper border.
- **Focus:** 3px ring (`#538ce94c` light, `#90a8ff59` dark). Disabled at 45% opacity.
- **Danger:** Alert Crimson on white — signed-in destructive only.

### Cards / Containers
- **Feature card:** Large radius, Frost Card (or 7% white on the dark canvas), Mist Line or 12% white border, card-lift, 1.5rem padding. Icon well 2.5rem. No hover translation.
- **Auth card:** Same large radius, Frost Card, standard hairline, stage-lift, 1.5rem padding, width `min(28rem, 100%)` (36rem when wide). Always paper, even on the navy canvas. Centered title at 1.5rem / 700.
- **CTA band:** Large-radius navy gradient (`#1e3a8acc` → `#00236fcc`), 15% white border, stage-lift, 2rem / 1.5rem padding, inverted primary inside.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Full-width, Input White, Mist Line, medium radius, 0.625rem 0.75rem, 0.9375rem body, resting lift.
- **Label:** Uppercase Quiet/Harbor Slate, 0.6875rem, 600, 0.06em.
- **Focus:** Chart Blue border plus the 3px focus ring.
- **Disabled / readonly:** Quiet Gray, Quiet Slate.
- **Error:** Alert Crimson at 0.875rem.

### Navigation
- **Public header:** Wordmark only (product name). Text links 0.9375rem / 500, medium-radius hit area, 2.5rem min height. Compact primary for “Demander un compte”. Theme toggle is a 2.5rem medium-radius square. Below 640px, text links hide; the wordmark, toggle, and compact primary remain.
- **Skip link:** Medium radius, primary fill, parked above the viewport, revealed on focus.
- **Signed-in (lower priority):** Night Dock sidebar, frosted topbar, Night Beacon on the active item.

### Wordmark
Public chrome is the product name in Wordmark Inter. No photograph, no crest tile, no secondary logotype. The login photograph may appear only as the hero illustration.

### Theme toggle
A 2.5rem medium-radius control. Sun and moon are stroke icons; one shows per theme. On marketing chrome it borrows the canvas border and icon-well fill.

### Named Rules
**The Wordmark Rule.** Public chrome is the product name. Never the VRP.jpeg photograph as a header mark.

**The Shared Control Rule.** Actions use the shared primary / secondary button pair. Do not invent a parallel button class.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set public chrome as the Inter wordmark at 1.125rem / 700.
- **Do** invert header and hero primaries on the dark canvas; keep the CTA-band primary light-on-navy in every theme.
- **Do** use Inter at 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 from fonts.bunny.net on marketing and app layouts.
- **Do** treat `VRP-login.jpg` as a hero illustration (large radius, stage lift), never as a mark.
- **Do** keep a skip control that moves into view on focus.
- **Do** isolate marketing regions from signed-in section, list, and header treatments.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use `VRP.jpeg` as a header mark, favicon stand-in, or brand tile on public pages.
- **Don't** place a kicker, eyebrow, or category line above the hero title.
- **Don't** invent testimonials, customer logos, pricing, or a second brand mark.
- **Don't** treat the signed-in shell as the design-priority surface.
- **Don't** introduce a parallel button class beside the shared primary / secondary pair.
- **Don't** use zero-offset halo shadows or a second display typeface.
